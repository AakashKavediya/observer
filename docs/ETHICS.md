# Observer Ethical Framework & Neurodivergent Affirming Principles

Observer is built on the conviction that cognitive overload and task paralysis are executive functioning realities, not moral or productivity failures.

---

## 1. Absolute Non-Negotiables

### 1.1 Non-Pathologizing & Non-Diagnostic
- Output is always framed as an **ESTIMATE** with associated confidence, never as a clinical diagnosis, medical evaluation, or psychological assessment.
- The interface explicitly reminds users: *"Observer provides local behavioral estimates, not a clinical diagnosis or medical evaluation."*

### 1.2 Zero-Shaming Language
- Words like *"lazy"*, *"unproductive"*, *"failed"*, *"distracted"*, or *"slacking"* are strictly barred from the codebase and user interface.
- No judgment-inducing gamification metrics exist: no productivity streaks, no guilt-based point deductions, no peer comparisons, and no personal scores.
- Language is always validating, gentle, and warm (e.g. *"Settled"*, *"Focused"*, *"Might be stuck"*, *"Feeling a lot"*).

### 1.3 Prioritize False Negatives Over False Positives
- **Core Rule**: Annoying an overloaded or hyperfocused individual with unwanted interruptions is far more harmful than missing an occasional freeze moment.
- Inferences require sustained evidence (dwell time hysteresis ~90s). If signals are ambiguous or lighting is poor, Observer defaults to silence (`UNKNOWN` or suppressed).

### 1.4 Non-Intrusive, Optional Interventions
- All check-ins and offers are:
  * **Silent by default**: Never emit chime alerts, pings, or sound effects unless specifically requested.
  * **Non-Blocking**: Never steal keyboard or mouse focus, never lock the screen, and never display modal popups that block underlying work.
  * **Dismissible**: Always easy to dismiss in a single click or keyboard stroke.
  * **Non-Flashing**: Strict adherence to low-stimulation visual guidelines with zero strobe or rapid animation effects.

### 1.5 Unconditional User Agency
- Users can pause camera monitoring with one click at any time.
- Users can delete all stored calibration data and settings instantly.
